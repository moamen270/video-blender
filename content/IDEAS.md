## 2. Three approaches — my recommendation is C

**A. Evergreen parody series only.** Recurring formats with famous characters (Batman, Vader, FNAF…).
Steady, safe, but no timing spikes.

**B. Trend-chasing.** Episodes timed to releases (Street Fighter film, CoD, Hunger Games, Halloween).
Bigger spikes, but we must ship on a date — hard until our pipeline is fast.

**C. Hybrid — recommended.** Three recurring series as the backbone (A) + one timed episode per
release/season when our pipeline can make the date (B). Start with episodes our current assets can
already make, so the first uploads test the *writing and hooks*, not the pipeline.

Recurring series (all in the channel tone — deadpan, serious characters, petty problems):
1. **Villains at Work** — iconic villains in mundane jobs (DMV clerk, IT help desk, party clown).
2. **Heroes Off Duty** — heroes defeated by everyday things (self-checkout, pickle jar, flat-pack furniture).
3. **Casting Call** — our own recurring duo (the Shapeshifter actor + the tired director) audition famous
   characters by theme each episode. Original cast + famous guests = our brand grows while we borrow reach.
4. **Standoff Etiquette** — an epic duel that never happens because the fighters argue about petty rules
   (reuses the samurai kit; western and sci-fi variants later).
