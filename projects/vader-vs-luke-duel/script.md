# Luke, I Am Your WHAT? — script (phase 2, gate G2)

**Series:** Star Wars parody (Vader & Luke; can recur) · **Length:** ~24 s, loop · **Tie-in:** evergreen
**Cast:** Vader, Luke (parody) · **Set:** a Death Star–style corridor (lit panels, not a black void), plus a bare
bulb hanging on a pull cord between them (in shot, unlit, from frame 1: planted for the payoff)
**Hook text (= title = cover):** "Luke, I am your WHAT?" · **Ending:** a real loop. The last frame is frame 1 (blades crossed).

Rules: skill `hook-script`. Frame 1 is mid-action and sound starts in 0.3 s. The payoff is a new surprise (the
lamp, then the reveal finally lands, then the roles swap).

**Deliberate beats (held pauses):**
- the finger hold (0.5 s);
- the silence after the sabers go off (1.0 s);
- the beat before "...Wait a minute." (0.6 s);
- the swing of the lamp after the click (0.8 s).

**Performance notes:**
- **Vader:** three registers. Grand (frame 1), angry shouting (the clash), then deadpan (the dark and the lamp). The joke is how calm and domestic he becomes: "It's so dark now." "That's better."
- **Luke:** sincere throughout, never sarcastic. He really can't hear, and he really can't see.

**The dark section:** it is never flat black (border check, and viewers). A dim blue room light plus the chest lights, then the warm lamp. The dark part lasts about 6 s.

**Sounds (real recordings only):**
