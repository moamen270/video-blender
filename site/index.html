Dummy Sticky

Serious faces. Ridiculous problems. Dummy Sticky makes short animated comedy videos (3D and stickman parodies) and publishes them on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

The Dummy Sticky publisher

The "dummy-sticky" app is a private tool used only by the Dummy Sticky channel owner. It lets the owner:

  • publish the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own accounts, and
  • read the performance numbers of those videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to decide what to make next.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or content.
