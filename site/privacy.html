Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This policy covers the "dummy-sticky" app (the Dummy Sticky publisher), a private tool used only by the owner of the Dummy Sticky channel to publish the channel's own videos and to read the statistics of those videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Who uses the app

Only the Dummy Sticky channel owner signs in to the app, with the channel's own accounts. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the channel's own accounts (account id, display name, avatar, follower counts).
  • The list of the channel's own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) and, where the platform provides it, audience-retention and watch-time statistics.
  • The video files and captions the owner chooses to publish.

The app does not access other people's accounts, private messages, contacts or any data of viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure how they perform. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared. Video statistics are kept in the channel's private production records. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access stops all data collection; stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the Dummy Sticky Facebook page.
